Khud se sawal





Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Mujhse dhyan nahi hota, mann bhaagta rehta hai.

Yahi normal hai. Mann ka kaam hi bhaagna hai. Dhyan mann ko rokna nahi, use bhaagte hue dekhna hai. Jis pal tumne dekha ki mann bhaag gaya, wohi jagna hai.

Kitna time roz dena chahiye?

Roz 5 minute, lagataar, 1 ghante ke ek din se behtar hai. Chhota shuru karo, jab mann kare tab badhao.

Kya dhyan ke liye dharm ya guru zaroori hai?

Nahi. Yeh sirf dhyaan dena sikhna hai. Koi bhi, kisi bhi vishwas ke saath, ya bina kisi vishwas ke, baith sakta hai.

Zorba the Buddha ka matlab kya hai, sach mein?

Zindagi ko poori tarah jeena (Zorba) aur saath mein poori tarah jaage rehna (Buddha). Tyaag aur bhog mein se ek chunna nahi, dono ko jaag ke saath jeena.

Kya dhyan therapy ki jagah le sakta hai?

Nahi. Dhyan madad karta hai, par gehri takleef mein kisi mental health professional se zaroor milo.
